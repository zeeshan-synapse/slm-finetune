Welcome to Board of Intermediate Education Karachi
URL: https://www.biek.edu.pk/Enrolment-College-Instructions.asp
Source: https://www.biek.edu.pk/default.asp

1. Please open the web portal https:/institute.biek.edu.pk,
which is specially designed for institutions.
2. It will ask the user id and password, please enter user id
and password, which will provide you by our IT Section secretly.
3. When you logged
in; please verify/approve the students, which enrolment forms
that you have received and make sure that all students’
pictures, Matric Data, Group/Faculty, and College name and other
documents are according to the required format that have been
attached by the students and they are genuine and verified.
(Incorrect format of pictures, selfies and other forge documents
will not be entertain by the BIEK and deem to be cancelled)
4. As per the Board’s
schedule or announcement of dates of enrollment; the portal
will be active, and the colleges must verify all the submitted
enrollment forms and check all the credentials of their student.
5. Institution must
have to approve the form of each student on Enrolment Portal.
The list of enrollment must be generated and download from the
web portal with QR Code and completed in all respect and signed
by concerned Principal/Head Master/ Head Mistress in triplicate
(Accounts, Enrolment, IT) submitted along with fee vouchers/slip
(if required) in the account section of the Board.
6. As per the directive
of Honorable Chief Minister Sindh, the Enrollment fee of the
all Government Students have been waived off.
7. The Enrollment
fee of Private colleges @2100 per candidate in shape of consolidated
pay order in favor of the Secretary Board of Intermediate Education,
Karachi.
8. No Enrollment
Form shall be entertained from any institution without affiliation
from Board of Intermediate Education, Karachi.
