1. Enrolment form is available now online at official website of the Board https://www.biek.edu.pk
2. Student have to follow the below procedure:
a. Click on Online Enrollment Web Portal systemb. If you are new, then please register yourself first with Email and Password and remember for further corrections and changes.
c. When you register, please update your profile.
d. After completion of profile, please click on the enrolment button
e. Enter your credentials/data in the required fields according to matriculation details.
f. Attach your picture (passport size with blue background), matric mark-sheet and college admission receipts and upload it.
g. If you are from Other Board then you must have to submit/upload Permission from the BIEK and also Migration Certificate from concerned Board.
h. If you are O-Levels / International Student then you have to take the equivalency from IBCC and upload instead of Migration.
i. Finally the system will show one PDF file of Enrolment form, please download it and print it.
j. Kindly submit this Printed Enrollment form as Hardcopy at your concerned college.
