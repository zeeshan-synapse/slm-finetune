Why Hiring Video Is the Future of Recruitment (5 Tips)?
A hiring video is a short, fun video that businesses make to attract applicants, communicate their culture, and explain why they are a great place to work. Consider it as the initial handshake of your company with a future employee. And just like a first impression, it needs to count.
The employment market has never been more competitive. The current candidates are no longer seeking a paycheck; they are seeking the right environment, the right team, and the right fit. In less than two minutes, a well-designed hiring video provides them with all that.
Still not convinced? Spark Hire claims that the number of applications to a video-based job posting is 34 times higher compared to no video. That in itself ought to have everything you need to know.
This article will discuss why a hiring video should be a part of your company’s strategy, five tips that are practical when it comes to creating a successful hiring video, a real-world case study, and answers to the most frequently asked questions that recruiters have when it comes to getting started.
Why A Hiring Video Should Be A Part Of Your Company?
Put yourself in the position of a candidate. They are browsing through tens of job ads, which are all similar. An effective hiring video will instantly win their interest and provide them with an actual impression of your company. It makes your brand relatable to people, which is impossible with a bullet-point job description.
Likewise, video creates trust in a much shorter time span than text. Candidates feel more reassured to apply when they can watch real people discussing their jobs and their working environment. LinkedIn claims that the engagement rate of candidates is two times higher when companies use video to recruit. One can hardly overlook that.
However, it’s not just about attracting more applicants. It’s about attracting the right ones. An effective hiring video establishes the expectations at the beginning, so there will be fewer inappropriate hires, and the entire process will be much smoother.
5 Tips For Creating The Perfect Hiring Video
Keep Short And Sweet
To begin with, no one would like to see a 10-minute corporate production. The optimal length of a recruitment video is 60-90 seconds. You wish to provide the candidates with enough to be excited, not too much to become bored.
Get to the point quickly. Give an introduction about your company, talk about what is exciting about the position, and maintain the momentum. Brief, forceful, and to the point always prevails.
Include Important Information And Answer Candidates’ Questions
Candidates have questions before they even apply. What is the job? How about the team? How do you describe the working environment? These are the things that your hiring video ought to address even before they can ask.
Discuss the basics: the position, the team makeup, the place of work or work-from-home arrangement, and any special benefits. I have witnessed companies lose numerous great candidates because they did not make the basics clear at the very beginning. Don’t make that mistake.
Show Your Company Culture By Showcasing Employees At Different Levels
This is where the majority of hiring videos go wrong. They feature one executive giving a polished speech — and candidates see right through it. Rather, present actual employees at various levels of the company with their authentic experiences.
To illustrate this, have a junior team member discuss growth opportunities, a mid-level employee discuss day-to-day life, and a manager talk about what they love about leading their team. That diversity creates a sense of authenticity and provides candidates with a full picture of what it is like to work there.
Create Different Videos For Different Roles
There is no one-size-fits-all. A video that is targeted at a software engineer should not be the same as one that targets a sales professional. The words, the tone, everything, should be suited to the particular audience you are attempting to reach.
One time, I was working with a company that made one generic recruitment video and applied it to all their vacancies. The results were mediocre at best. As soon as they began to produce role-specific videos, the quality of their applications increased dramatically. Personalization matters — even in recruitment.
End It With A Call To Action
Lastly, do not leave the candidates in doubt as to what to do next. Finish your video with a definite and straightforward call to action. Show them where to apply, what the next step looks like, and how long the process is.
Wyzowl states that 84 percent of the population have been persuaded to take action after viewing a video by a brand. Take advantage of that momentum to push applications. Something as simple as “Apply now at [website], we’d love to hear from you” goes a long way.

FAQs
What should I say in a hiring video?
Pay attention to the mission of your company, the duties of the position, group culture, and other important benefits. Make it natural and informal. Aspiring employees prefer to be told the truth rather than corporate lingo.
What makes a hiring video successful?
A successful hiring video is short, authentic, and specific. It includes actual workers, responses to typical job interview questions, and concludes with a strong call to action. The high production value is an advantage, though the authenticity is more important.
What are the don’ts in making a hiring video?
It should not be too long, too scripted, or too company-centered as opposed to the experience of the candidate. Do not just have executives, but get voices throughout the organization. And do not omit the call to action at the end.
