The Three-Engine Architecture
One Unified Platform
Most platforms are built as a single block. Conversaction AI™ is architected as three specialized microservices that work in sync, so every part of the system does exactly what it was designed to do, at scale.
Laravel Backend Service
It manages your API, handles multi-tenant database isolation, and locks down security via OAuth 2.0. It’s the traffic controller that ensures every request is routed safely and instantly.
Python AI Workflow Engine
The "thinking" layer. Built on FastAPI and LangGraph, this engine uses agentic logic gates to do more than just chat, it decides when to book a meeting, trigger a Jira ticket, or hand off to a human.
Web Scraper Service
The automated brain. This service continuously crawls your site and docs, feeding updates directly into the AI's knowledge base. If your policy changes on your site, the AI learns it in real-time.
