Our Product
The Intelligence Layer Behind Modern Business Success
At Synapse, products aren’t built as isolated tools. Each solution acts as part of a shared intelligence layer designed to validate inputs before action, apply defined decision logic, execute within clear boundaries, and log outcomes for review and audit.
This is how automation stays reliable, and businesses move work forward across hiring, customer interaction, operations, and IT.
AI systems designed to act from day one with structure, control, and measurable outcomes.

In addition to our suite of pre-built AI products (like iRecruit One, Agentic Bot, Opira AI, Conversaction AI, and Cyber Security Automation), Synapse Tech Inc. offers full custom software development, engineering custom web platforms and mobile apps tailored to your workflows.

Products designed for private AI deployment, such as Opira AI, can run completely offline, on-premises, or on private cloud infrastructure to protect data privacy and sovereignty.
