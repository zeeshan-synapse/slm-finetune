The Automation Power-Stack
Open, scalable, and enterprise-ready technologies.
Great automation isn’t just about picking a tool, instead it’s about how your systems talk to each other when things get busy. We don’t believe in “duct-tape” fixes. That’s why we build clean, dependable environments that kill off repetitive busywork and give you actual visibility into your data.
Our automation stack consists of four proven technologies: N8N, Make.com, Airbyte, and Airtable. Each of them solves a different part of the operational puzzle while working seamlessly together.
N8N – Open-Source Workflow Automation
Self-hosted workflows built for security, flexibility, and scalability.
N8N acts as the “neural core” for organizations that cannot compromise on privacy. Because n8n is self-hosted and fair-code, we build complex, “shift-left” logic that lives entirely within your firewall.
Deploy n8n when your enterprise needs more than just simple triggers. It’s perfect for custom Python scripts and agentic decision-making that needs to stay local. You get the power of advanced automation without ever handing your sensitive data over to a third party. It’s great when you need full control and zero privacy compromises.

Airbyte — The Industrial Data Pipeline
Clean, consistent data automatic syncing across the tools your business depends on.
Moving data between tools is usually a mess, but Airbyte turns it into a solved problem. We use it to architect resilient ELT pipelines that pull information from your production databases or disparate apps and drop it into a central warehouse, clean, secure, and fully auditable. It handles the high-volume grunt work so your data is actually where you need it, when you need it, without the constant maintenance headaches.
⚙️ Accurate, reliable ELT pipelines
🔐 Clean datasets for AI and forecasting
📊 Synchronized databases, apps, and reporting systems.
🗄️ Analytical dashboards
Airtable — Structured Workflows Without Complexity
A flexible workspace built for real operations, providing shared visibility across projects and processes.
An autonomous system is only as good as its memory. We use Airtable as the flexible, relational backend for your workflows. Airtable helps businesses replace scattered spreadsheets, emails, and manual trackers with a single, structured workspace.
It allows your AI agents to retrieve information, log actions, and provide your team with a clear, human-readable view of your automated world. Hence, creating clarity across teams, and reducing confusion.
