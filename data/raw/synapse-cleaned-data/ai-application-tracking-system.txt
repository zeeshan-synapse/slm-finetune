How AI Application Tracking Systems Actually Work?
Let’s understand how the AI application tracking system actually works. It’s not just a single click. There is a whole logic and science behind it. Thus, let’s uncover this in very simple words.
1. NLP (Natural Language Processing)
Natural Language Processing is like a human in code. It is because NLP reads resumes the way a human does. Also, it reads and understands context, skills, and seniority. This is the step that happens when your resume passes through an AI application tracking system.
2. Machine Learning Models
A machine learning model helps the application to check and analyze the successful hirings of the past. It uses the same parameters to hire the next candidate. In simple words, past hirings are the data for ML to hire the next candidate.
3. Automated Resume Screening
Automated resume screening does nothing, but it ranks the applicants based on their qualifications, experience, skills, and knowledge about the respective field.
4. Chatbots & Engagement Tools
Suppose you have applied to a job and got the reply from a “no_reply” email. Then, you already know how chatbots and engagement tools work. They are trained by a model to reply to you. This does not just end here. They even answer your questions, schedule interviews, and keep in touch with applicants.
5. Predictive Analytics
Predictive analytics is a full intelligence stack. This is something that HR teams benefit from. Because it is best in accuracy, speed, and assures you a higher chance of hiring the right candidate.

Major Benefits of Using an AI ATS
There are numerous benefits of an AI application tracking system. Here we will go through some of them.
Speed & Efficiency
Undoubtedly, AI is superior in speed to a human. In the same way, trained models are efficient enough to make the right decision based on statistics. According to an international report, AI cuts resume screening time by up to 70%.
Quality of Hire
As we discussed earlier, the ATS system evaluates an applicant on many bases. As a result, a company gets the right candidate for the required task.
Bias Reduction
AI can’t be biased, but a human can be. ATS ensures the applicant’s skills and qualities as long as the models are audited properly.
Stronger Candidate Experience
When a candidate applies for a position in a company, they want to hear from them as soon as possible. So, Automated updates and chatbots are there to keep applicants engaged. This step improves the experience of a candidate with that company.
Strategic Insights
ATS shows every single detail of a candidate on a screen. It shows all the positive and negative aspects of a candidate. As a result, it gets super easy to choose the perfect candidate.
Risks, Ethical Concerns & Challenges
We all know that every technology comes with some drawbacks as well. No doubt, AI-ATS systems are powerful, but they are not perfect as well. You must know all the aspects when you are going to use them. Here are some of the challenges mentioned below:
It doesn’t give you the proper results if they are not audited completely.
ATS lacks transparency if the AI model is a “black box.”
It ignores the candidate perception
It may be costly for smaller teams
If you are using AI, then here is something you need to remember. AI should only be supporting decisions. It should not replace judgments.
