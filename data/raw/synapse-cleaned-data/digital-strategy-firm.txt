What Makes A Digital Strategy Firm A Great Option For You?
Not all consultancies are experienced with digital transformation. But there are some that have a proven methodology to guide you through this process. Here’s what they do.
They Conduct A Detailed Digital Assessment
A great firm does a digital assessment before starting to build. They’ll look at what needs to be replaced, what’s missing, and what can be automated or augmented by artificial intelligence. Understanding your starting point is critical to creating a transformation strategy.
They Set A Clear Goal And Vision With You
The next thing is they don’t give you a cookie-cutter solution. They engage with your executive team, comprehend your future business objectives, and build a step-by-step technology strategy to achieve them. For instance, one company may need to use AI in customer service, while another needs to completely revamp data management.
They Have A Customer-Centric Mindset
The best companies always have a focus on the customer – your customer or your employee. They understand customer experience and pain points, and develop technology that can make the interaction with your company better.
What Benefits Will You Get With A Digital Strategy Firm?
Building Relationships That Will Last
Digital strategy firms don’t just deliver a strategy. They establish an ongoing relationship, continuously adjusting their strategy to fit your evolving business needs and the changing technological environment. This becomes one of the most important assets that you can build.
Better Operational Efficiency
With the right technology roadmap, your organisation stops doing things that are time-consuming and routine. McKinsey reports that companies fully embracing digital transformation have 23% higher profits than other companies. The payoffs from the increased efficiency alone can be well worth the cost.
Saving Big With Smart Solutions
Digital transformation is not cheap. Technology platforms, systems, and the timing for AI are all prime examples of choices that can cost you years. Hiring a digital strategy firm ensures you don’t make those missteps and start with the right technology decisions.
Adaptability To Market Changes
The tech landscape is ever evolving, and so are your competitors. A good firm builds flexibility into your plan, so your business can easily adapt to new AI technologies or changing market conditions, rather than being forced to respond to these changes.
Cons
Cultural Resistance
Digital transformation is as much about people as it is technology. Your team may push back on new processes, procedures, or technologies. And even a sound plan can falter on the people front if there’s no robust change management in place.
Too Much Invested In Technology
Other firms are too zealous about new technologies and propose overly complicated solutions. Ensure the firm you work with focuses on practical, scalable solutions rather than slick products that don’t fit your business needs.
Challenges In Integration And Implementation
Execution can go awry even with a great strategy. The biggest challenge of any transformation is integrating new digital technologies with existing systems.
