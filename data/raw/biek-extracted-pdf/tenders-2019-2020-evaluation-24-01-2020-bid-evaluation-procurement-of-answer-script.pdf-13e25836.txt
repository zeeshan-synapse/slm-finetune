The Committee was informed that a tender notice was got published in leading
newspapers; Daily Tribue Express (English) as on 17.11.2019, Daily Jang (Urdu) as on
18.11.2019 and Daily Awami Awaz (Sindhi) as on 17.112019, The same was also hoisted/put
on the website of BIE Karachi and SPPRA on NO. T00535-19-0006 as on 18.11.2019.
According to SPPRA Rules, single stage - two envelopes bids, containing Technical and
Financial Proposals, were invited for the purpose.
In response to advertisement, four (4) firms participated and submitted their Bids;
comprising of Technical & Financial Proposals. The Technical proposals were opened on
02.12.2019 and as a result of technical evaluation; following three (3) firms were found
technically qualified:
i) M/s. Mighty Deals, Karachi.
ii) M/s. Benison Printers, Karachi.
iii) M/s.Sana Printers (Pvt) Ltd.
Accordingly, the Procurement Committee opened the Financial Proposals of the
technically qualified bidders in presence of bidders or their representatives. The amount of Bid
Security and the item-wise rates quoted by firms; were read-out / announced loudly. A detailed
comparative statement; showing article-wise rates was prepared and is annexed with the
minutes at “A”. The required bid security (@ 3% of bid price) was found in tact in respect of
both the firms, as follows:
a) M/s. Mighty Deals, Karachi. (Pay Order No. 13119787 dated Rs. 1,000,000.)
b) M/s. Benison Printers, Karachi. (Pay Order No. 06994880 dated 02.12.2019
Rs. 901,000/-).
©) M/s. Sana Printers (Pvt) Ltd (Pay order No.1986161-2 dated 19.11.2019 Rs.341,670).
Keeping in view the necessity of required articles, Committee emphasized to complete
the delivery of stores within stipulated period of Sixty days, as also defined in the bidding
document and decided that the respective Inspection Committee must ensure the inspection of
stores in accordance with the samples of articles approved by the Procurement Committee
After thread-bared discussions, the Committee decided as follows:
DECISIONS:
1) Committee decided that the contracting firms must be ensured the delivery
of stores items stipulated time as mentioned in the bidding documents.
2) Committee decided that before the supply of store items, the contracting
firms must be inform in writing to conduct the inspection of answer script in
accordance with the samples approved by the Procurement Committee.
3) On the basis the lowest offered rates of individual articles, the Procurement
Committee recommended to award of the contract to technically qualified
bidders, which as follows:
