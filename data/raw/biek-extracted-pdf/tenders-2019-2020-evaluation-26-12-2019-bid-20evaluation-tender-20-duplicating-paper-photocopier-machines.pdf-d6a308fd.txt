Meeting started with recitation from Holy Quran. The Committee was informed that the Bids
invited through press for procurement of duplicating paper, master roll & ink for Riso Graph & Photo
Copier Machines were opened on 26.09.2019. Committee reviewed the description of bill of quantities,
which has been mentioned “Duplicating paper reams containing 500 sheets in each 50-60 grams,
super quality, smooth finish good texture strictly without any variation in any ream under any
circumstance subject to satisfactory acceptance & printing on our Riso Graph Machines.”
According to which the 04 samples containing different codes were sent to the technical expert through
the Controller of Examinations, to use the duplicates paper and submit his findings to Procurement
Committee to finalize for award of contract.
In accordance with decision of the Procurement Committee dated 26.09.2019, was referred to
the technical expert under the supervision of Controller of Examinations shall examine the samples
with regard to material, durability, polishing, gramage, finishing and quality and subject to satisfactory
acceptance & printing on Riso Graph machine and report be submitted to Procurement Committee to
finalize the award of contract.
vv
Accordingly, technical expert under the supervision Controller of Examinations has carried-out
the evaluation of articles supply by the bidding firms and submitted their findings to the Procurement
Committee for further necessary action, the contents of such is reproduced as under:
The samples of Duplicating Papers & Master roll received from the Admin
General Section through the secret codes, according to which the printing foreman
and G.T.X Operator has check the duplicating paper and master ink through the
code No.9, be procured and to provide to the Examinations Department, for the
‘year 2020, in order to smooth examination process.
The committee first reviewed the specification required by the Board and also given bidding
documents and thoroughly examined & evaluated the samples furnished by the bidders one by one. It is
further informed that in last year the duplicate papers have been procured, and supplied to the
confidential Section during the examination machine operators has informed the duplicating papers are
upto mark and as per approved samples but our machine was not done the exercise properly, resulting
they were facing so many problems, in view of situation, committee has opined the report of technical
experts is very important for us, because they are end users, so that we totally rely on the their report.
The Procurement Committee appreciated the efforts made by the committee to evaluate the
samples of articles with regard to specifications mentioned in the tender documents and reviewed his
recommendations.
After thread bared discussion, the committee finally decided, the duplicating papers, master roll
and ink be procured, as per the technical expert report submitted the Controller of Examinations, which
