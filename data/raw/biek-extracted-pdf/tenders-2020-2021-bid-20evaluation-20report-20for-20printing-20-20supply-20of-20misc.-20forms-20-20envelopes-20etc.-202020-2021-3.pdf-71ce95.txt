Meeting started with the recitation from Holy Quran. The Committee was informed that sealed bids
were invited through press for Printing & Supply of Misc. Forms & Envelopes etc. for the Board of
Intermediate Education Karachi was published in leading newspapers; Daily Express Tribune (English) as on
September 26, 2020 as well as Daily Express (Urdu) as on September 27, 2020 and Daily Awami Awaz
(Sindhi) as on September 28, 2020. The same was hosted on the website of BIE Karachi as well as SPPRA at
Sr. No. 700535-20-0005 dated 01.10.2020. According to SPPRA, Rules, Single stage - One envelope bids,
containing Technical and Financial Proposals, were invited forthe purpose.
Inresponse to advertisement, three (09) interested firms purchased the tender documents out of which
(06) interested firms submitted their bids (Attendance Sheet annexed at‘A’) which are as follows:
1) Mis. Al Faisal Enterprises
2) Ms. Bin Sajad
3) Ms. Vorson
4) Mis. Benison Printers
5) Mis. Shan Printing Press
6) M/s. Ubaid Enterprises
The bids were opened by the Procurement Committee as on 15.10.2020 in presence of the
representatives of Bidding Firms and the rate quoted by bidders was announced loudly one by one. Committee
preliminary evaluated the bid technically with regard to availabilty of required documents; viz. NTN and Sales
Tax Registration, business experience and dealing of firms in supply of similar stores, committee has satisfied
after preliminary evaluation of the documents submitted by the bidders except M/s. Vorson.
Page 1 of3
Furthermore, the bids of Mis. Vorson has been declared disqualified by the Procurement Committee
after assessment of ‘documents due to non fulfilment of eligibility criteria mentioned in bidding documents.
Keeping in view of the necessity of required articles, committee emphasized to complete the delivery of
stores within stipulated period as ‘mentioned in the bidding document, and decided that the respective
Inspection Committee must ensure the inspection of stores in accordance with the samples of articles approved
by the procurement Committee.
The detailed comparative statements for items were prepared by the Committee is attached with
minutes as Annexure “B”.
The Committee unanimously recommended awarding the contract to the best evaluated bid / lowest
quoted rates.
DECISION:
The Committee recommended awarding the contract for printing & Supply of Misc.
Forms & Envelopes etc. on the basis of lowest quoted rates & best evaluation to the
bidders as follows:
