The IT. Related Procurement Committee informed that the sealed bids were invited for Procurement
of Computers, Printers & Scanners et. for the Board of Intermediate Education Karachi through publication
Of NIT in leading newspapers; Daily Business Recorder (Engish) as on 18-12-2024 as well as Daly Jang
{Urdu) as on 19-12-2024 and Daily Kavish (Sindh) as on 21-12-2024. The same was hosted onthe website of
BIE Karachi as well as EPADS at Sr. No.S-241251447. According to SPPRA, Rules, Single stage - One
envelope bids, containing Technical and Financial Proposals, were invited for the purpose.
In response to advertisement, Eight (08) interested fms purchased the tender documents and all of
them submitted their bids (Attendance Sheet annexed at “A") which are as follows:

and Sales Tax Registration, business experience and dealing of firms in similar work. After preliminary
evaluation of the documents submitted by the bidders, the following firms have been qualified
Eligbilty/Qualifcation Report as well as Comparative Statement, has been prepared by the LT. Related

After thread barred discussion, in view of past experience with brand of Brother Printers, the
7. Related Procurement Committee has recommended that, item No.3 & 5 of Comparative Statement be
procured on the basis of best evaluation and reputation in the market as far as concern to other printer to be
dropped off due to various reasons such as non-availabilly of parts and higher rates of cartridge.
In view of above, the I.T. Related Procurement Commitlee unanimously recommended awarding the
contract to the technically qualified bidders and on the basis of best evaluated & lowest quoted rates.

market as far as concern to other printer i.e. item No.2 to be dropped off due to various
reasons such as non-availability of parts and higher rates of cartridge and all other items
