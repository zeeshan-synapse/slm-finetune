7. Scope of Work / Technical Specifications
Bidders must supply, installation & configuration plan / diagram, deployment time, test and commission
all listed items. All accessories, cabling, racks, patch panels, switches, UPS and other components must
be provided with installation, labeling, tagging and complete schematic diagrams and warranty or
support.
8, Delivery and Installation
Delivery and installation at BIEK site within 60 days after issuance of contract award (extendable by
mutual consent)
9. Warranty and Maintenance
All equipment must be covered by manufacturer's warranty. Vendor to provide one-year maintenance
services with defined response times and spare part availability.
10. Payment Terms
+ 100% payment upon complete delivery and acceptance within 30 days.
+ Part payment allowed only if approved by Chairman BIEK.
+ Taxes, levies, and stamp duty to be borne by Supplier.
11, Support Contract with Resource
‘+ After completion of warranty period the procuring agency will do the support contract with the
successful bidder for one year with resource engineer 24/7 in office hours for any installed
network and its accessories issues rectifications. The contract may be extended according to the
performance and service level provided.

ARTICLE VI - WARRANTY & MAINTENANCE
All equipment supplied shall carry manufacturer’s warranty. Supplier shall provide one-year
maintenance services post-installation at no additional cost.
ARTICLE VI — PAYMENT TERMS
100% of the Contract Price on complete delivery and acceptance within 30 days on submission of
invoice supported by Acceptance Certificate. Part payment on part supply only when approved by
Chairman BIEK. Deduction of income/sales tax at source as per law.
ARTICLE VIII — LIQUIDATED DAMAGES & PENALTIES
In case of delay, liquidated damages @ 0.5% per day of the contract value up to a maximum of 10%
shall be imposed. Inferior or substandard items will be rejected; Security Money may be forfeited and
Supplier may be blacklisted.
ARTICLE IX — VARIATION / ADDITIONAL WORK
BIEK may, at any time during performance, request changes in scope. Any price/time adjustments shall
be mutually agreed in writing before execution.
ARTICLE X - INTEGRITY PACT
Supplier undertakes that no bribe, commission, gratification, or kickback has been given or shall be
given. Any false declaration will make the contract void; Supplier shall indemnify BIEK for any
loss/damage incurred on account of corrupt practices.
ARTICLE XI- DISPUTE RESOLUTION
Any dispute arising under this Agreement shall be amicably resolved; if unresolved, it shall be referred
to arbitration in accordance with the Arbitration Act, 1940 and SPPRA Rules. Venue: Karachi, Pakistan.
Language: English.
ARTICLE XII — TERMINATION
BIEK may terminate this Agreement by giving 15 days’ written notice if the Supplier fails to execute
the job according to the requirements. In the event of termination, Performance Security shall be
forfeited
ARTICLE XIII - INDEMNITY
‘The Supplier shall indemnify and keep BIEK harmless from and against all damages, losses, claims or
expenses caused by any defect in the goods, negligence, omission or misconduct of the Supplier.
ARTICLE XIV — NOTICES \
All notices under this Agreement shall be in writing and sent by registered mail/courier to the addresses,
of BIEK and the Supplier.
