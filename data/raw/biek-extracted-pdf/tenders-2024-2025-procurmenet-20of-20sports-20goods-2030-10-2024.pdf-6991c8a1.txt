MINUTES OF THE MEETING OF PROCUREMENT COMMITTEE HELD ON
30.09.2024 AT 03.00 PM TO OPEN THE BIDS INVITED FOR

30-09-2024 at 03.00 p.m. in the Committee Room of Board of Intermediate
Education Karachi, under the convener ship of Audit Officer, BIE Karachi to open
the bids invited for Procurement of Sports Goods for the Board of Intermediate

The Committee was informed that bid were invited through hoisting of NIT on
Board's & EPADS website Sr. No. $:240930457 dated 13-09-2024 for Procurement of
Sports Goods According to SPPRA, Rules, Single Stage - One Envelope bids,
containing Technical & Financial Proposals, were invited for the purpose.
In response to hoisting of NIT, only one interested firm purchase the tender
documents and submitted online bid on EPADS Attendance Sheet annexed at ‘A’.
The bid was opened by the Procurement Committee on 30.09.2024 in
presence of the representative of Bidding Firm and the rate quoted by the bidder
was announced. Committee preliminary evaluated the bid technically with regard
to availability of required documents; viz. NTN and Sales Tax Registration, business
experience and dealing of firms in supply of similar stores, committee has satisfied
after preliminary evaluation of the documents submitted by the bidder. (Eligibility
Report as annexed at 'B')

Committee was informed that as per SPPRA rules-48, “even when o}
bid Is submitted, the bidding process may be considered valid, eieectia ee

Committee prepared a confronting rate chart (as annexed at ‘C’) of the quoted
items with a view of last awarded contract. After detailed discussion, the
Procurement Committee was approved the quoted rates.
It is stated that Convener of Procurement Committee was suggested to
opted the Director Physical Education, BIE, Karachi as member (being technical
expert and end user) to evaluate samples provided by the bidder so that the
committee could be decided on consideration on merit.
Accordingly, the Director Physical Education has carried-out the evaluation
of samples one by one and informed that all samples are acceptable and upto
mark in accordance with the specification mentioned in the tender documents.
Procurement Committee appreciated the efforts made by Director Physical
Education with regard to evaluation of samples.
After detailed discussion, the Procurement Committee was considered
offered made by M/s. Sindh Enterprises.
DECISION:
Considering the last awarded contract and also the prices offered by the
bidders, the Procurement Committee recommended awarding the contract
of Procurement of Sports Goods to M/s. Sindh Enterprises, Karachi amounting
to Rs.2,112,620/-(Rupees Twenty One Lac Twelve Hundred Six Hundred Twenty
Only).
The meeting ended with a vote of thanks to and from the Chair.
