Meeting started with the recitation from Holy Quran. The Committee was informed that sealed bids
were invited through press for Hiring of Furniture for Examination Centers Annual Exams - 2021 for the
Board of Intermediate Education Karachi was published in leading newspapers; Express Tribune (English) as
on February 26, 2021 as well as Daily Express (Urdu) as on February 26, 2021 and Daily Pak (Sindhi) as on

In response to advertisement, Five (05) interested firms purchased the tender documents out of which
Four (04) firms submitted their Technical & Financial bids (Attendance Sheet annexed at “A”).
™
Accordingly, the Technical Proposals were opened by the Procurement Committee as on 24.03.2021 in
presence of the representatives of Bidding Firms. Committee preliminary evaluated the bid technically with
regard to availability of required documents; viz. NTN and Sales Tax Registration, business experience and
dealing of firms in services in relevant field, after preliminary evaluation of documents M/s. Nayab enterprises
has disqualified due to non fulfillment of eligibility criteria and following firms were found technically qualified
(Eligibility / Qualification Report annexed at “B”):
1) M/S. New Ghazi Decoration Service
2) MIS. Zaib Enterprises
3) MIS. New Al Razzag Enterprises
Keeping in view the Technical Qualification requirements, the Committee found that the technical
proposals of above firms “Responsive” to further compete in bidding process, hence decided to open their
Financial Proposals being technically qualified at a later date:
After thread-bared discussions, Committee decided and recommended as follows:
Committee declared the bids as “Responsive” submitted by above firms as that
qualified the Bid Evaluation Criteria and decided to open their financial proposals at a
later date to finalize the recommendation of award contract.
The meeting ended with a vote of thanks to and from the Chair.

2. M/s. Zaib Enterprises (Pvt) Ltd
3. M/s. New Al-Razzaq Enterprises (Pvt)
