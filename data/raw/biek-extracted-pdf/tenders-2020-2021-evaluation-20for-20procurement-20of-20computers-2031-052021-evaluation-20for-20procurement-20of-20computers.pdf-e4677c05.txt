‘A meeting of the Procurement Committee BIE Karachi was held on 24.02.2021 at 03.00 p.m. in the
Committee room of Board of Intermediate Education Karachi, under the Convener ship of Audit Officer BIE
Karachi, to open the Technical Proposal invited for Procurement of Computers & |.T. Related Items for the

Meeting started with the recitation from Holy Quran. The Committee was informed that sealed bids
ere invited through press for Procurement of Computers & I.T. Related Items for the Board of Intermediate
Education Karachi was published in leading newspapers; Daily Business Recorder (English) as on February 02,
2021 as well as Daily Jang (Urdu) as on February 06, 2021 and Daily Awami Awaz (Sindhi) as on February 05,
2021. The same was hoisted on the website of BIE Karachi as well as SPPRA at Sr. No. T00535-20-0014
dated 10.02.2021, According to SPPRA, Rules, Single stage - Two envelope bids, containing Technical and
Financial Proposals, were invited for the purpose.
In response to advertisement, Six (06) interested firms purchased the tender documents out of which
(05) firms submitted their Technical & Financial bids (Attendance Sheet annexed at EAN):
Accordingly, the Technical Proposals were opened by the Procurement Committee as on 24.02.2021 in
presence of the representatives of Bidding Firms. Committee preliminary evaluated the bid technically with
regard to availability of required documents; viz. NTN and Sales Tax Registration, business experience and
dealing of firms in services in relevant field, after preliminary evaluation of the documents all firms were found
technically qualified by committee (Eligibility / Qualification Report annexed at “B”).
