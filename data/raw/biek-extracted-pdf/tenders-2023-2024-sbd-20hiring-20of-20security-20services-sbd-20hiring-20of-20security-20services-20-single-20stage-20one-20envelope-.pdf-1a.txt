a) Deputy Secretary Admin General , calls tenders for the Hiring of Security Services in

each item of work and estimate amount and all other data called for shall be given.
g) “In case of a discrepancy between the unit price and amount entered for any item in
the schedule of quantities, the unit price will be taken as correct and the amount will
be adjusted accordingly when the tender is being examined”.
