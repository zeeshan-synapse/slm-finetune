INSTRUCTIONS
(1)
To acquire Migration Certificate for Regular Students attestation of form from the Principal of College
concerned is a must.
(2)
Attachment of Photocopy of Enrolment Card/Registration Card and Admit Card or Marks Sheet is
essential.
(3)
It is pointed out that Migration Certificate once issued shall not be issued again until and unless details
of lose of Original Migration are provided to the Board, hence, its care is essential. Moreover,
Migration Certificate shall only be issued to Original candidate only.
(4)
Urgently required document shall be issued on the date given by the office. Under Normal Fee,
required document shall be issued after 15 working days, ignoring holidays and, if, last day is a holiday
then it will be issued on next working day.
(5)
Form must be filled in carefully and completely. Incomplete forms shall not be accepted.
(6)
All required documents must be collected within 30 days of the issuance date, after that these
documents will be discarded & candidate will have to submit a new form along with prevalent fee.
NOTE: In case of giving incorrect information or filling in form wrongly a new date shall be given by
the office.
In case of issuance of Duplicate Migration Certificate, the
candidate will have to let this office know that when, where
and how his/her Original Migration Certificate lost.
