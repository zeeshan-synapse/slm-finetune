The members of the Committee has unanimously agreed in principal that the bid was invited through
hoisting of NIT on Board's & EPADS website Sr. No. S- 241241134 for Supply of Uniforms & Shoes According to
SPPRA, Rules, Single Stage - One Envelope bids, containing Technical & Financial Proposals, were invited for the
purpose.
In response to hoisting of NIT, Two (02) interested firms purchase the tender documents and submitted
their bid on EPADS and marked their attendance manually at the time of opening of bids (Atlendance Sheet
annexed at ‘A’).
The bids were opened by the Procurement Committee and evaluated the bid technically with regard to
availabilly of required documents; viz, NTN and Sales Tax Registration, business experience and dealing of firms
in supply of similar stores.
Furthermore, after assessment of documents, due to non-fulfillment of eligibility criteria mentioned in
bidding documents, the following firm has been declared as Non-Responsive (Eligibility Report as annexed at ‘B'):
