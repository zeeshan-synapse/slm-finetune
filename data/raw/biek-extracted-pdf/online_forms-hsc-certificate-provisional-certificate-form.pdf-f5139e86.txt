INSTRUCTIONS
1. All columns need to be filled in completely and correctly. Incomplete form shall not be accepted.
2. Duly filled form must be accompanied with Photostat copy of Marks Sheet and
Enrolment/Registration Card.
3. Provisional Certificate is issued only to successful candidates. Provisional Certificate is issued once,
therefore, its safety is the responsibility of the candidate. Further, Provisional Certificate will be
collected by the original candidate.
4. Provisional Certificate is not issued after the declaration of date of issuance of Original Certificate.
5. In case of giving incorrect information or filling in form wrongly a new issuance date shall be given by
the section concerned.
6. All required documents must be collected within 30 days of the issuance date, after that these
documents will be discarded & candidate will have to submit a new form along with prevalent fee.
Forms can be downloaded from the website of BIE Karachi i.e. www.biek.edu.pk
