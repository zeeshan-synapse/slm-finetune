queries in writing within three calendar days, provided they are received at least five calendar

shall not be accepted.

procuring agency shall visit the Godown(s)/Store(s) of bidder(s), for checking the quality

Price” shall be awarded the Contract subject to Visit Report of the Godown/Store of
bidder(s).
