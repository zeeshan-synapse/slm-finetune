ON 18.11.2021 AT 3.00 P.M., TO OPEN THE BIDS INVITED
FOR PRINTING OF TABULATION REGISTERS 2021-2022
A meeting of the Procurement Committee BIE Karachi was held on 18.11.2021 at
3.00 p.m. in the Committee Room of Board of Intermediate Education Karachi, under the
Convener ship of Audit Officer, BIE Karachi, to open the Bids invited for Printing of Tabulation
Registers for the Board of intermediate Education Karachi. Following attended the meeting:

Meeting started with the recitation from Holy Quran. The Committee was informed
that sealed bids were invited through press for Printing of Tabulation Registers was
published in leading newspapers; Daily DAWN (English) as on October 10, 2021 as well as
Daily Jang (Urdu) on October 10, 2021 and Daily Sobh (Sindhi) as on October 11, 2021. The
same was hoisted on the website of BIE Karachi as well as SPPRA at Sr. No. T00535-21-0005
dated 18.10.2021. According to SPPRA, Rules, Single stage - One-envelope bids, containing
Technical andFinancial Proposals, were invited for the purpose.
response to advertisement, six (06) interested firms purchased the tender
dogfnents and five (05) of them submitted their bids (Attendance Sheet annexed at “A’}
ich are as follows:
1) M/s. Benison Printers, Karachi
2) M/s. Ubaid Enterprises, Karachi
3) M/s. Mighty Deals, Karachi
4) M/s. Sana Printers, Karachi
5) M/s. Shan Printing Press, Hyderabad
The bids were opened by the Procurement Committee on 18.11.2021 in presence of
the representative of Bidding Firms and the rate quoted by bidders was announced.
Committee preliminary evaluated the bid technically with regard to availability of required
documents; viz. NTN and Sales Tax Registration, business experience and dealing of firms in
supply of similar stores, committee has satisfied after preliminary evaluation of the documents
submitted by the bidders and announced all of them Responsive in Bid Qualification Report
(as annexed at “B") with referred to the criteria mentioned in bidding documents
The committee first reviewed the specification required by the Board and also given bidding
documents and thoroughly examined & evaluated the samples furnished by the bidders one by one
